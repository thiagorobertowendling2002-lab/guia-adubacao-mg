# Surface brief: index.html (Guia de Adubação MG)

## Scope and mode

One page, mode Operate with a playful brand. A visitor types a soil analysis, picks a crop and a planting date, and leaves with doses, a dated plan and the manual's management advice.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

Producers and field technicians in Minas Gerais with a lab report in hand, mostly on a phone outdoors. Plain words with the manual's real terms, no formulas. Every number cites a manual section and page; estimates are labelled. Static, offline-capable, no emoji, results area flat and legible.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Saco de Adubo (fertilizer bag), chosen by the user from the decision round as Impeccable's pick. The memorable moment: the result lands as a printed bag label, with giant N, P and K roundels, and each dated task is thumped on as a red lot stamp.

## Unresolved

Final fonts files and the exact set of hand-drawn crop emblems are decided during the build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The recipe for the soil printed as the bag it would come in: the label is the answer. Refuses the category default of a card dashboard with leaf icons and a green gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Screenprinted bag graphics. Bag-yellow field, deep field-green ink and frames, signal red for stamps and alerts, bone white reading ground. Heavy expanded grotesk for names and numbers, condensed uppercase for stamps and tags. Thick ruled panels with a stitched seam, woven-sack hatch only on the top and bottom bands, roundels for N, P and K, guarantee-table rules, red lot stamps slightly rotated. Flat colour, hard offset shadows, no gradients, no glass.

STORY: The visitor sees that the manual has become a bag per crop, picks theirs, reads their own soil as a guarantee table, and gets a label saying how much, plus a MODO DE USAR with dates. They believe it because each line names its manual page. They act by following the stamped dates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band in bag yellow with the title in heavy expanded type and a stitched seam below it. Directly under it, "Qual saco você vai abrir?" with four bags standing like a shelf row (Café, Milho, Feijão, Frutas), each with a hand-drawn emblem and its name. A progress belt of three stitched stops sits above the shelf. The primary action, the green Continuar button, sits bottom right of the active panel.

FORM: Saco de Adubo, position 1 of 7 on my ordered list (picked by the user from the round), seed key 9accdcf4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
